.. _listeners:

Listeners
=========

Listeners let users define actions to take
in response to changes in the monitored elements, such as
a change in the deployment layout or the arrival of new
statistical data.

There are two kinds of listeners:

- :ref:`listeners_domain_listener` acts upon changes in the DDS entities
  of the deployment and upon the arrival of new statistical data.
- :ref:`listeners_physical_listener` acts upon changes in the physical entities
  of the deployment.
